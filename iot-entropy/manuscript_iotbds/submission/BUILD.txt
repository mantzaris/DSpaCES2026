Anonymous Regular Paper source package

Run in this directory:
  latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error paper.tex

Needs pdflatex, BibTeX, latexmk and the standard TeX Live packages
newtxtext/newtxmath, booktabs, graphicx, microtype, hyperref and T1 fonts.
Official unmodified llncs.cls v2.25 and splncs04.bst are supplied.
Output: paper.pdf. No files outside this directory are needed.
