Anonymous Regular Paper source package

Run in this directory:
latexmk -pdf -jobname=paper -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex

Output: paper.pdf. Requires pdfLaTeX, BibTeX, latexmk, newtxtext/newtxmath,
T1 fonts, booktabs, graphicx, microtype, url, hyperref and standard LNCS
dependencies. Official llncs.cls v2.25 and splncs04.bst are supplied.
All manuscript inputs are local; no experiment or generator is needed.
