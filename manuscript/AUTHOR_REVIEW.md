# Substantive author review

This is a compiled, visually reviewed case-study draft, not a submission-ready or
novelty-certified paper. Author metadata matches the supplied name, affiliation
and email. The author should decide the following before submission:

1. **Contribution strength.** Is the three-way separation and evidence-linked
   failure diagnosis sufficient for a full case study? No new inference theorem,
   adaptive advantage, capacity advantage or operational deployment is claimed.
   A publication decision remains a scientific judgment, not a passed code test.
2. **Exploratory evidence.** Accept or further narrow conclusions based on only
   four development weeks, reused backgrounds, synthetic disturbances and repeated
   development investigation. Fresh seeds are not sealed real-world confirmation.
   The current authorization does not permit opening the sealed outcomes.
3. **Decision meaning.** Detection uses lag0-6 inclusive and miss delay7, with
   any-channel/update alarms. M3b's added affected-meter requests occur later;
   the revised explanation does not prove a faster policy would succeed. Confirm
   that this is the desired operational diagnostic rather than a claim about
   physical outages or interventions.
4. **Uncertainty and information contract.** Review the limited registered-query
   guarantee, aggregate/fine replacement, future-support scoring convention and
   omitted selection history. Raw and empirically adjusted intervals under-cover.
   Retained state and consumer wire estimates must not become total-resource or
   network-deployment claims in the abstract, talk or cover letter.
5. **Venue and release.** Confirm current presentation/no-show/anonymity details,
   registration and any waiver before author-controlled submission. Verify that
   public release of the existing code/numerical artifacts and the final author
   block is appropriate. Dataset attribution is retained; raw data are not added.

No additional experiment, policy tuning, author contact, submission or pod
lifecycle action is implied by these review items.
